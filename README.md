# Hospitality Revenue Insights Dashboard

## Overview

The hospitality industry relies heavily on data-driven decision making to optimize revenue, improve room utilization, and enhance customer experience. However, raw booking data alone does not provide meaningful insights unless it is transformed into actionable business metrics.

In this project, I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze hotel performance across multiple properties, cities, room categories, and booking platforms. The dashboard consolidates operational and financial data into a single view, enabling stakeholders to monitor business performance and identify growth opportunities.

## Business Problem

A hotel chain operating across multiple cities needed better visibility into its overall performance. While large volumes of booking data were available, management lacked a centralized system to answer critical business questions such as:

* Which properties generate the highest revenue?
* Are available rooms being utilized effectively?
* How efficiently are bookings converting into actual stays?
* Which booking platforms deliver the best results?
* How do pricing strategies impact revenue generation?
* Which properties require operational improvements?

Without clear visibility into these areas, it becomes difficult to optimize pricing, improve occupancy, and maximize revenue.

## Business Metrics Used for Analysis

To address these challenges, the dashboard was built around key hospitality KPIs commonly used by hotel management teams.

### Revenue

Measures total income generated by hotel bookings and serves as the primary indicator of business performance.

### ADR (Average Daily Rate)

Evaluates the average revenue earned per occupied room and helps assess pricing effectiveness.

### RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)

One of the most important hospitality KPIs, combining occupancy and pricing performance to measure how efficiently available inventory generates revenue.

### Occupancy Percentage

Measures the percentage of available rooms that are occupied, providing insight into room utilization and demand patterns.

### Realisation Percentage

Tracks how many successful bookings actually result in completed stays, helping identify revenue leakage caused by cancellations and no-shows.

### DSRN (Daily Sellable Room Nights)

Represents the total room inventory available for sale.

### DBRN (Daily Booked Room Nights)

Represents the number of room nights successfully booked by customers.

### DURN (Daily Utilized Room Nights)

Represents the room nights that were actually occupied by guests.

Together, these metrics provide a comprehensive view of operational efficiency, customer demand, pricing strategy, and revenue performance.

## Solution

To transform raw booking data into meaningful business insights, I designed an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

* Monitor revenue performance in real time
* Track occupancy and room utilization trends
* Evaluate pricing effectiveness through ADR and RevPAR
* Compare hotel performance across locations
* Identify high-performing and underperforming properties
* Analyze booking platform effectiveness
* Monitor realization rates and cancellation impacts
* Support data-driven strategic decision making

## Key Insights Generated

The dashboard helps uncover several important business insights:

* Revenue contribution across different hotel categories
* Performance differences between individual properties
* Occupancy trends over time
* Pricing effectiveness through ADR analysis
* Revenue efficiency through RevPAR monitoring
* Booking platform performance comparison
* Realisation rate trends and cancellation impact
* Room inventory utilization through DSRN, DBRN, and DURN analysis
